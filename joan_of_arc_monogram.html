Joan of Arc Monogram

6" x 6", Typography I

The results of an early Famous Figres monogram project using the initials of Joan of Arc. It comes in two variousations, one black and the other in gold.

The primary challenge of this project was to represent something as comlpex as a person through something as simple as a handful of letters. I ultimately decided to represent her fanatical religious nature both through the dramatic, golden color that reflects the concept of the light of God, and the use of the "O" shape as a sort of halo in the monogram.

Created in Illustrator.
