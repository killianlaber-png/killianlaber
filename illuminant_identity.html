Illuminant Identity

1200PX x 210PX, Visual Communication

The logo signature for a bookstore company called Illuminant. Includes single color and color reversal variants, as well as a business card.

The ultimate goal behind this logo design was to modernize something old. I took inspiration from illunimated manuescripts for the concept of the company, so a candle as the primary logo element seemed a good fit. The premise was that it would be specifically catered to night readers, selling booklights and shiny-texted books alongside the standard inventory, further connecting the medieval inspiration to the modern application.

Created in Illustrator.
